Questions

Frequently Asked

Is Fawaky completely natural?expand_more

Yes. No artificial colours, no preservatives, no essences — just real Ice Apple (Nungu), tender malai and Nata de Coco cubes.

What are the Nata de Coco cubes?expand_more

Chewy coconut-gel jelly cubes that add a uniquely satisfying texture to every sip — a Fawaky signature.

Does it contain added sugar or preservatives?expand_more

No preservatives, ever. Most of the sweetness comes from the real fruit itself — we add just a light touch of sugar to balance the taste, and nothing more.

How should I store it?expand_more

Keep chilled and refrigerate after opening. Best served cold, straight from the cooler.

How do I order, and do you supply cafés?expand_more

Order any time on WhatsApp. For bulk and café / restaurant supply, message us for B2B pricing and regular delivery.
